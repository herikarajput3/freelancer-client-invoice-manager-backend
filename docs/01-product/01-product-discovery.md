# Problem Statement

Independent software developers often manage clients, invoices,
payments, notes, and follow-ups across disconnected tools such as
spreadsheets, notes, email, and PDF templates. This fragmentation
increases mental load and reduces confidence in running their business.

The product exists to become the trusted source of truth that helps
freelancers organize their business and make better decisions.

# Product Principles

1. **One Source of Truth** — Every important business detail has one trusted home.
2. **Reduce Mental Load** — The system remembers business information so users don't have to.
3. **Support Decisions, Not Storage** — Features should help users decide what to do next.
4. **Simplicity Over Completeness** — Focus on solving the core problem exceptionally well while keeping business behavior explicit and consistent.
5. **Relationships Are Business Assets** — Clients are long-term relationships, not just contacts.
6. **Historical Integrity** — Once financial records become part of business history, they must remain accurate and preserve their original business context.
7. **Business Before Technology** — Business behavior is defined by approved business rules rather than implementation decisions.

## Scope Clarifications

The MVP focuses on establishing a reliable business workspace rather than providing a full accounting solution.

The product is intended to:

- Manage a single freelancer business per workspace.
- Preserve historical financial records once they become part of business history.
- Support long-term client relationship management alongside financial tracking.
- Provide Business Pulse as a read-only summary derived from business records.

The MVP intentionally excludes accounting, tax compliance, multi-business workspaces, and implementation-specific concerns.

# Risks

## Critical

- Users stay with spreadsheets.
- Product appears to be another invoice app.
- Business Pulse does not become a habit.

## Medium

- Relationship notes are not valued.
- Freemium conversion is weak.

# Key Assumptions

- Freelancers want one trusted business workspace.
- Business Pulse becomes part of a regular workflow.
- Relationship history influences future client decisions.
- Simplicity is more valuable than feature completeness.
- Historical financial information should remain trustworthy after it has been recorded.
- Business rules should remain consistent regardless of how the software is implemented.
